## How does Pomade Paste lay down waves?
Pomade Paste lays waves down with a smooth, whipped texture that melts into the hair and provides hold without grease. Scoop a dime‑sized amount, warm it between your palms, and work it in from root to tip. Brush it through with a curved boar‑bristle brush to set the pattern, then tie a durag to lock the style overnight. The shea butter and aloe vera add moisture while the beeswax and soy wax give the hold that keeps waves in place all day.

## How does Pomade Paste define curls and coils?
For curls and coils, the same paste defines each strand without weighing it down. After warming a small amount in your hands, finger‑style it through damp or dry hair from root to tip. The avocado oil adds a natural shine, while the aloe vera and shea butter keep curls hydrated. Build more hold by adding a little extra paste and re‑working the sections. The result is defined, bouncy curls that stay intact without flakes.

## How does Pomade Paste keep a beard line clean?
A clean beard line comes from the paste’s ability to grip hair without stiffness. Warm a pea‑sized amount, then smooth it down along the jawline and cheeks. The beeswax and soy wax provide a flexible hold that tames flyaways, while the grape seed oil and vitamin E condition the beard. Brush through with a boar‑bristle brush for an even finish. The paste washes out clean, so there’s no buildup on the skin.

## How does Pomade Paste hold a slick‑back?
A slick‑back stays sharp because the paste delivers a firm, glossy hold that resists humidity. Apply a generous amount to damp hair, comb it back from the forehead to the crown, and smooth the sides. The emulsifying wax and stearic acid help the product spread evenly, while the lemongrass and geranium essential oils leave a subtle scent. The hold lasts through the day and rinses out without residue.

## How does Pomade Paste secure a bun?
For a bun, the paste adds enough grip to keep the base tight while allowing movement. Warm a modest amount, work it through the gathered hair, and twist into a bun. The soy wax and beeswax create a tacky texture that holds the shape, and the shea butter prevents the hair from feeling dry. A quick brush over the surface smooths any stray hairs. The style stays put until you decide to take it down.

## What is the routine for using Pomade Paste across these styles?
The routine stays consistent no matter the style. Start with clean, damp hair or beard. Scoop a small amount of Pomade Paste, warm it between your palms, and distribute from root to tip. Choose your tool: brush for waves, fingers for curls, comb for slick‑backs, or simply smooth for beards and buns. Finish with a durag or a light mist of water to set the look. Repeat daily for a clean, intentional process.
