## What makes Pomade Paste work for waves?
The paste lays waves down with a smooth, lasting hold.  
- Scoop a dime‑sized amount and warm it between your palms.  
- Work it in from root to tip.  
- Brush through with a curved boar‑bristle brush to set the pattern.  
- Add a little more if you need extra hold, building gradually.  
The result is a defined wave pattern that stays in place all day.

## How does Pomade Paste define curls?
The same light paste separates and shapes each curl without weighing it down.  
- Warm a small amount in your hands.  
- Finger‑style the product through damp or dry curls.  
- Let the curls air‑dry or diffuse for added definition.  
- Re‑apply a touch more for tighter coils.  
Curls stay hydrated, shiny, and frizz‑free.

## Can Pomade Paste keep a beard line clean?
Yes — the paste smooths stray hairs and holds a sharp beard edge.  
- Apply a pea‑sized amount to fingertips.  
- Work it into the beard from root to tip.  
- Shape the line with your fingers or a brush.  
- The hold lasts through the day without stiffness.

## What’s the best way to style a slick‑back with Pomade Paste?
A slick‑back needs a firm, glossy finish that the paste provides.  
- Start with damp hair for easier distribution.  
- Warm a generous scoop between palms.  
- Comb the product back from the hairline to the crown.  
- Use a brush to smooth any flyaways.  
- Add a second layer for extra shine if desired.  
The style stays sleek from morning to night.

## How do you use Pomade Paste for a secure bun?
The paste gives enough grip to hold a bun without crunch.  
- Gather hair into a high or low ponytail.  
- Warm a small amount and smooth it over the tail.  
- Twist the tail into a bun and secure with pins.  
- Lightly brush the surface for a polished look.  
- The hold remains flexible, so the bun moves naturally.

## How do you build hold without grease or flakes?
The whipped texture melts cleanly, leaving no residue.  
1. Start with a dime‑sized scoop.  
2. Warm fully in your hands until it turns translucent.  
3. Apply evenly from roots to ends.  
4. Style as needed — brush, finger‑comb, or smooth.  
5. If more hold is wanted, repeat with a second thin layer.  
Each layer adds control while the finish stays matte‑shine and flake‑free.
