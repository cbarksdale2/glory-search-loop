## How does Pomade Paste work for waves?
The paste lays waves down by providing a light, pliable hold that follows the brush pattern. Warm a dime‑sized amount between your palms, work it from root to tip, then brush with long, steady strokes from the crown outward. The beeswax and soy wax give the hold, while shea butter and aloe vera keep the hair moisturized. For deeper wolfing phases, add a little more paste and repeat the brush session.  

## How does it define curls?
Finger‑style the paste into damp or dry curls to enhance definition without crunch. The avocado oil adds a natural shine, and the glycerin helps retain moisture. Scoop a small amount, warm it, then rake it through sections from root to tip. Let the curls set or diffuse for extra volume. The result is defined, touchable curls that stay in place all day.  

## How does it keep a beard line clean?
A thin layer of paste smooths stray hairs and shapes the beard line. Warm a pea‑sized amount, apply it to the beard, then use the curved boar‑bristle brush to lay the hairs down. The hold from beeswax and soy wax keeps the line sharp, while shea butter and aloe vera condition the skin underneath. For more detail, see [Why is Pomade Paste good for beards?](https://glorybyus.com/blogs/the-process/why-is-pomade-paste-good-for-beards).  

## How does it hold a slick‑back?
Apply a generous scoop to damp hair, comb it back, and let the paste set. The combination of beeswax, soy wax and stearic acid creates a firm yet flexible hold that resists humidity. The shine from avocado oil and grape seed oil gives a polished finish without looking greasy. Re‑apply a small amount if you need extra control later in the day.  

## How does it secure a bun?
After gathering hair into a bun, smooth the paste over the surface and edges. The hold locks flyaways in place, while the moisture from shea butter and aloe vera prevents the hair from feeling dry. A light brush over the bun distributes the product evenly. The bun stays neat through workouts or long meetings.  

## What is the routine for using Pomade Paste across styles?
1. Start with clean, damp hair or beard.  
2. Scoop a dime‑sized amount (adjust for length and density).  
3. Warm the paste between palms until it turns translucent.  
4. Work it in from root to tip — brush for waves, finger‑style for curls, smooth for beard, comb back for slick‑back, or coat the bun.  
5. Finish with the Glory durag or a light mist of water if you need extra lay.  
Repeat daily or as needed; the paste washes out clean with the Rosemary Myrrh Shampoo & Body Bar.

## Frequently asked questions
### Will Pomade Paste make my hair greasy?
No. The formula is designed to give hold and shine without leaving a greasy residue. It washes out clean with shampoo.  

### Can I use it on a fresh cut?
Yes. The paste is gentle enough for a fresh fade and firm enough to lay down hair while you’re wolfing.  

### Does it contain any animal‑derived ingredients?
The paste includes beeswax, which is animal‑derived. All other ingredients are plant‑based oils and butters.  

### How much should I use for a full head of waves?
Start with a dime‑sized amount. Add more in small increments until you reach the desired hold.  

### Is the product suitable for coils?
Yes. The same finger‑styling method works for coils, providing definition and moisture without flaking.
