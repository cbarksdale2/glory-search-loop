## What makes Pomade Paste different from traditional greases?  
Traditional greases often leave a heavy, shiny film that can feel sticky. Pomade Paste is whipped and spongy, melting into an invisible hold with a clean, natural finish. It lays every stray hair down on command without the weight or cake that greases create.

## How do you apply Pomade Paste to a beard?  
Start with a dime‑sized amount, warm it between your palms to wake up the botanicals, then run it through the beard from root to tip. Use long, deliberate strokes to lock in the pattern and lay every strand down.  

- Scoop a small amount.  
- Warm between palms until it melts.  
- Work through beard, curls, or waves.  
- Brush to distribute evenly and set the style.  

## Which ingredients give the paste its hold and shine?  
The hold comes from beeswax, emulsifying wax, and soy wax, which create a flexible matrix. Shea butter and aloe vera gel provide moisture without weight. Avocado oil and grape seed oil add fatty acids that build texture and a natural shine. Vitamin E protects the hair shaft, while lemongrass and geranium essential oils give a fresh scent.

Yes. The paste works instantly on a fresh cut to define edges and on deep wave phases to maintain compression. Its all‑day hold adapts to whatever stage you’re in, keeping the pattern locked without re‑application.
