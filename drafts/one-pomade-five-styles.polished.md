## How do you lay waves down with Pomade Paste?
Work the paste in, then brush it through. Waves are about pattern, and pattern comes from consistent brushing.

2. Warm it between your palms until it melts.
3. Work it in from root to tip.
4. Brush it through with long, steady strokes from the crown outward to lay your pattern.

## How do you define curls and coils with it?
Finger-style it instead of brushing. Curls and coils want definition, not a flat lay, so your hands do the work.

- Warm a dime-sized amount between your palms.
- Work it through from root to tip, section by section.
- Twist or rake each section with your fingers to shape it.
- Need more hold? Add a little more and build from there.

Start small. It is easier to add a second pass than to take one away. The paste is light, so you can build in layers until the curls sit the way you want them, with shine and without grease.

## How do you keep a beard line clean?
Smooth it down, then brush it in. A beard looks sharp when every stray hair sits flat along the cheek and jaw.

1. Warm a small amount in your palms.
2. Smooth it down through the beard, following the direction the hair lies.
3. Brush with steady strokes to lay every strand down.

The curve on the brush follows your jaw as well as your head, so it does double duty. Because the paste washes out clean, a beard routine stays simple: style in the morning, rinse at night. ## How do you hold a slick-back or a bun?
Smooth it down and set the shape. The page puts it plainly: smooth it down for a beard, slick-back or bun.

**For a slick-back:**
- Warm the paste and work it in from root to tip.
- Smooth the hair back from the front to the crown with your palms.
- Press the sides flat and add a little more where you want more hold.

**For a bun:**
- Work the paste through before you gather the hair.
- Gather and secure the bun.
- Smooth your palms over the surface to lay down loose hairs at the edges.

The same rule holds here as everywhere else: start with a dime-sized amount, then build. You get hold and shine without the heavy, greasy look.
