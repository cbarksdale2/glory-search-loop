## How Glory sounds
- **Plain and direct.** Short sentences. Say the thing, then show how. ("One jar. Every texture.")
- **Speaks to "you", talks as "we".** Community first: the Glory Family.
- **Culture and lineage, without lecturing.** Hair as pride, rhythm, legacy; waves, durags, fresh cuts, the wave check.
- **The process matters as much as the product.** Routine words: ritual, intentional, process, clean, consistent.
- **Practical how-to in one breath.** "Scoop a small amount, warm it between your palms, and work it in from root to tip."
- **Confident, never clinical.** No lab talk, no medical promises.
- **Taglines used sparingly**: "Indulge in the Process." · "Our Hair Matters" · motto "Brush Your Hair". Sign-off for community pieces: "Welcome to the Glory Family."

## Never
- Medical or cure claims: treats, cures, regrows, prevents, heals, hair loss, growth, follicles, circulation.
- "Plant-based" for the pomade (it contains beeswax). Use "healthy hair care" or "clean ingredients" as the brand does.
- Other brand names. Invented reviews, stats, experts or quotes.
- Emojis, hashtags, all-caps shouting, "game-changer", "revolutionary".
- Hair-loss, thinning, transplant or other health topics.
